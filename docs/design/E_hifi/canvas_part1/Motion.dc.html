nelioMotion with GSAP

Everything moves with purpose.

These boards play the motion with CSS keyframes that mirror the GSAP timelines. In the app, GSAP (useGSAP + ScrollTrigger) runs them. Nothing loops forever except idle agents and floating phones, and everything turns off for people who prefer reduced motion.
