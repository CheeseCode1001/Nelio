Rules

Flat colour only
No gradients, glows or glass. Fun comes from colour blocks, stickers, illustrations and bold type.
Light by default
Dark is available with full parity. Users choose Light, Dark or System.
Blue means go
One blue (or ink CTA) primary action per view. Focus ring is 3 px blue.
Pink means Nelio AI
Anything the AI did or suggests carries pink. No vendor names, ever.
Status = icon + word + colour
Never colour alone.
Honest states
“In development”, “Coming later”, “Confirming with Instagram”. No fake success.
