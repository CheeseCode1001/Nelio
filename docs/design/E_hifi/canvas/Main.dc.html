Rules of the look

Flat colour only
No gradients, no glows, no glassmorphism. Fun comes from colour blocks, bold type and playful agents.
Volt means go
Volt is reserved for the primary action, active state and focus ring. One volt button per view.
Grape means AI
Anything Nelio AI or an agent did or suggests carries grape.
Status never by colour alone
Every status chip has an icon and a word. Orange/blue pairs are used over red/green.
Honest states
“In development”, “Coming later”, “Confirming with Instagram”: never fake success or progress.
Touch and focus
Targets ≥ 44 px. Focus = 3 px volt outline with 2 px offset. Respects reduced motion.
