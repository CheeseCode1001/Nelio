Rules

Text never overflows
Titles clamp at 2 lines, meta and emails at 1 line with an ellipsis, chips never wrap and sit in rows that do. Long values wrap; numbers never break.
Flat only
Blur panels are a solid translucent ink (rgba 11,11,20 at ~50%) with backdrop blur, never a gradient scrim. Ticket strips and clip bodies are single flat colours.
Sizes
Grid cards 260–360 px wide, radius 28 (inner photo 20–22), padding 18, 12–16 px between parts. Equal heights in a row; the button sits at the bottom.
Mobile
Every concept works at 358 px: stats keep one line each, chip rows wrap, the action becomes full width where there are two.
