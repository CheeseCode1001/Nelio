Okay, stop budgeting like this. Seriously. Everyone tells you to write down every naira you spend, and nobody actually does that. So here is what works instead. Pay yourself first. The day your salary lands, move ten percent into savings before you touch anything else. Then spend the rest without guilt.
Now, the fifty thirty twenty rule. People love it, but it is a myth for most students. If your rent alone takes half your income, the rule breaks on day one. Start with what you can save, even if it is five percent, and grow it every month.
Why do savings fail? Because the money is too easy to reach. Keep your savings in a separate account with no card. If you have to wait a day to get it, you will think twice before spending it.
Last tip. Rent hacks that work. Split a bigger flat with two friends instead of renting a small one alone. You will save more than any budgeting app can find for you. That is it. Pay yourself first, ignore rules that do not fit your life, and make saving the easy choice.
